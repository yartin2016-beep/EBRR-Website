Examination Board of Rohingya Refugees

The Examination Board of Rohingya Refugees (EBRR) is a community-led educational initiative established to support standardized examinations, academic development, and educational opportunities for Rohingya students.

EBRR works with community-based schools and education stakeholders to strengthen examination practices, promote academic standards, and encourage students to pursue their educational goals.

Our work is guided by the belief that education is essential for building a peaceful, informed, and hopeful future.
