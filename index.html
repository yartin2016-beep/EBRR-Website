WHO WE ARE

Education deserves structure, quality and recognition.

The Examination Board of Rohingya Refugees (EBRR) is a community-owned, non-partisan educational body established by and for Rohingya people residing in the refugee camps of Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

EBRR brings Community-Led Schools together under a shared framework for educational coordination, systematic examinations and academic records. The Board is rooted in community participation and works to improve learning opportunities for Rohingya children and young people.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision and Mission

Guided by the aspirations and educational needs of the Rohingya community.

◎
OUR VISION

A generation empowered through education.

To build a high-quality, structured education system for all Rohingya children; expand access to secondary education and higher learning; preserve mother tongue, literature and identity; and prepare a generation of educated, skilled and responsible individuals.

✧
OUR MISSION

Coordinate, improve and recognize learning.

To coordinate Community-Led Schools, strengthen teaching and learning, organize standardized examinations and certification processes, promote Rohingya language and cultural identity, and protect equal access to education without discrimination.
